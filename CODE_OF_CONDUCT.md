# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or ability. We pledge to make participation harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, kind about people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Give credit and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, and sustained disruption of discussions are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository’s security contact or a direct message to a maintainer. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept confidential.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
